A hyper-realistic open-world defense survival game where your body evolves every level while intelligent enemy waves overrun Earth’s final safe zones.

Core Loop

  1. Spawn into a ruined open zone and gather resources.
  2. Choose a defense point and build traps or barricades.
  3. Survive escalating attacker waves with adaptive AI behaviors.
  4. Select one of three evolution cards after each wave.
  5. Extract for rewards or push deeper for higher risk.
